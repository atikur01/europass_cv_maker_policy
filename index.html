🔒 Privacy Summary at a Glance

Europass CV Maker is built with a privacy-first, offline-first architecture. All your resume data, personal details, uploaded photos, and generated PDF files remain 100% on your local device. We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or store your personal information on any external servers or third-party cloud databases.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Europass CV Maker ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal information you input while building your resume and curriculum vitae.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information, what device permissions are utilized, and your rights concerning your data in accordance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information Collection & Storage

Our application operates entirely offline on your device without requiring you to create an account or provide credentials.

A. Personal Information Processed Locally

When you create or edit a resume, you may enter the following data into the app:

  • Personal Identity: Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Nationality, Profile Photo.
  • Contact Information: Email address, Phone number, Website/Portfolio URL, LinkedIn URL, Physical address (Street, City, Postal Code, Country).
  • Professional Details: Professional Summary/Profile, Work Experiences (Job Titles, Employers, Dates, Descriptions).
  • Education & Qualifications: Degrees, Institutions, Fields of Study, Dates, Grades.
  • Skills & Competencies: Digital/Technical Skills, Mother Tongues, Foreign Languages & CEFR proficiencies, Driving Licences.
  • Additional CV Sections: Projects, Certifications, Publications, Volunteering, Awards, Memberships, Conferences, References, and Custom Sections.

Storage: All the above information is saved strictly within the private local storage sandbox of your device (in JSON format and cached image files). It is never transmitted across the network to our developers or any third party.

B. Automatically Collected Information & Telemetry

  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not embed trackers like Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Flurry.
  • No Advertising SDKs: We do not display third-party advertisements or integrate ad trackers (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads).
  • No Device Fingerprinting: We do not track your device's unique IMEI, advertising ID (GAID), or location.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

To provide full resume-building and export capabilities, Europass CV Maker requests certain Android system permissions. Each permission is used solely for its explicit user-initiated function:

Permission Category Purpose & Usage
CAMERA Optional / Media Allows you to take a photo using your device camera to crop and use as your CV profile picture. Only accessed when you explicitly tap the camera option.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Media & Files Allows you to pick an existing profile picture from your photo gallery or select a previously exported CV JSON file for backup restoration.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
(Android 9 and below)		 Storage Used on legacy Android versions to save exported PDF resumes and JSON backups directly to your Downloads or public Documents directory.

Note: You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > Europass CV Maker > Permissions.

4. Third-Party Services & Libraries

Europass CV Maker utilizes select open-source libraries to power PDF generation, image cropping, and UI rendering:

  • Google Fonts: Bundled/cached typography to ensure standardized, professional resume font styling.
  • Native System Share Sheet: When you choose to share your exported PDF or JSON file (via email, messaging, or cloud drive), processing is handed directly to Android's secure system share dialog and the destination application of your choice.

No third-party SDK has access to your resume contents through our application.

5. Data Retention & Deletion (Your Rights)

Because all data resides exclusively on your local device, you possess total control over your information at all times:

  • Editing & Deleting Individual CVs: You can edit or delete any CV entry directly from the application's dashboard. Deletion permanently removes the corresponding JSON file and associated photo from local storage.
  • Complete Data Erasure: You can wipe all app data at once by navigating to your Android Settings > Apps > Europass CV Maker > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Data Portability: You can export your data anytime as a standardized JSON backup file or as a printable/shareable PDF document.

6. Security of Your Data

We implement the following measures to ensure the security and privacy of your data:

  • Sandboxed App Directory: Your CV files and cropped photos are stored in Android's isolated internal storage directory, which cannot be accessed by other unprivileged apps on your device.
  • No Remote Vulnerabilities: Because there are no backend databases, user accounts, or server API endpoints, your CV data is not susceptible to remote server data breaches.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for job seekers, students, and professionals creating curriculum vitae. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or process personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you believe a child has created content on a shared device, all such data can be instantly removed by clearing app storage.
